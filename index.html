origin story

WHO IS OFC GAMERZ?

I was born on 20th January 2009, into a world that had no idea it was giving birth to a future gaming legend. From the very first cry, it was clear — this one was built different.

Growing up, while most kids were outside, I was inside mastering my reflexes, building strategies, and grinding to the top. My first controller felt like an extension of my hand — not a toy, but a weapon.

Today I dominate across multiple genres — from precision FPS gameplay in Valorant and CS2, to surviving until the last circle in Fortnite & PUBG, to building empires and causing chaos in GTA and Minecraft.

This website is my digital headquarters — where I share my journey, my stats, my streams. Welcome to the HQ.
